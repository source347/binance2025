The 4 Essential Steps to Buying BTC

  • Account Registration & Security

    Click the CTA button above to start the registration process. You can sign up using your email, phone number, or Google account. Once registered, immediately enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) using the Google Authenticator app. This is crucial for securing your future investments.

  • Identity Verification (KYC)

    Binance is a regulated exchange, requiring Know Your Customer (KYC) verification to allow you to trade fiat currency (like USD, EUR, etc.) and withdraw funds. This involves submitting government-issued ID (passport or driver's license) and a clear selfie. The process usually takes a few minutes and is required for security and legal compliance.

  • Funding Your Account (Deposit)

    Navigate to the Wallet section and select Deposit. You have two main options:

    1. Fiat Deposit: Transfer funds directly from your bank account (usually the cheapest but slowest) or use a credit/debit card (faster, but often carries a higher fee).
    2. Crypto Deposit: If you already own crypto on another platform, you can transfer it to your unique Binance wallet address.
    We recommend starting with a small fiat deposit to get familiar with the process.

  • Making the Purchase (Convert or Spot)

    Once your deposit is confirmed, you are ready to buy Bitcoin (BTC)! Binance offers two simple ways:

    • The "Convert" Tool (Easiest): This is ideal for beginners. Simply select the currency you deposited (e.g., USD) and the crypto you want (BTC). Enter the amount, and Binance gives you a simple quote. Click Convert to execute the trade instantly.
    • The "Spot Trading" Interface: This offers more complex options like limit orders, but for your first purchase, the Convert tool is far simpler and less intimidating.
    Congratulations, you now own Bitcoin!
